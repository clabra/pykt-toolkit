# ECTEL 2026 Abstract Proposal

**Title**: Grounding Deep Learning in Pedagogical Theory for Interpretable Knowledge Tracing

**Abstract**:
As Artificial Intelligence increasingly shapes the educational landscape, a challenge for Technology-Enhanced Learning is ensuring that innovation remains purposeful and grounded in learning theories. This is especially critical for Intelligent Tutoring Systems based on Knowledge Tracing, which can be classified as "high-risk" due to their role in assessing or monitoring learners. While deep learning models offer high predictive accuracy in KT, their "black-box" nature often undermines human agency and trust. To address this we propose a novel model termed *gTransformer*, that bridges the gap between high performance and pedagogical interpretability. 

Our approach employs *Representational Grounding*, a methodology that anchors the internal states of a Transformer-based architecture to established pedagogical constructs from Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT). By integrating theory-based parameters into input sequences and using attention mechanisms to capture longitudinal dependencies, *gTransformer* projects latent representations into enriched, student-specific parameters such as prior knowledge and learning rates. This ensures that the model’s reasoning is explicitly aligned with educational purposes and cognitive principles. 

Experimental validation on five benchmark datasets demonstrates that gTransformer achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, significantly outperforming traditional theory-based models with a minimal predictive cost relative to opaque deep learning architectures. By identifying individual learning signatures from complex interaction histories, *gTransformer* enables context-aware personalisation that is both student-centred and trustworthy. This work demonstrates a clear path towards 'Mindful TEL', where advanced AI tools are purposefully designed to empower educators through theory-grounded diagnostics, ensuring that technological innovation remains at the service of human learning.

**Keywords**: Deep Knowledge Tracing; Mindful TEL; Trustworthy AI; Interpretability; Personalisation
