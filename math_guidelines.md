# ✅ MDPI Mathematical Content Checklist (Author Use)

Use this checklist to validate **variables, symbols, equations, and mathematical environments** before submission.

***

## 1. Variables, Symbols, and Fonts

*   [ ] **Variables and symbols are italicized** in both text and equations  
    *(e.g.,* *x*, *y*, *f(x)**)*
*   [ ] **Words and numbers in equations are NOT italicized**
*   [ ] Formatting of symbols (**italic vs. roman**) is **consistent between text and equations**
*   [ ] Any **exceptions to italics** follow established field conventions

***

## 2. Subscripts, Superscripts, and Indices

*   [ ] **No spaces** around operators in subscripts/superscripts  
    *(e.g.,* *aₙ₊₁*, not *aₙ + ₁*)\*
*   [ ] **No spaces** in spectral or index subscripts unless needed for clarity  
    *(e.g.,* *E₃₆₅nm*)\*
*   [ ] Subscripts/superscripts are formatted consistently throughout

***

## 3. Operators, Relations, and Spacing

*   [ ] **Spaces around operators** between two quantities  
    *(e.g.,* *a = 2b*)\*
*   [ ] **No space** when symbol applies to a single quantity  
    *(e.g.,* *>50*, not *> 50*)\*
*   [ ] **Ratios have no spaces**  
    *(e.g.,* *1:100*)\*
*   [ ] Trigonometric functions include a space before the argument  
    *(e.g.,* *cos Θ*, *sin Θ*)\*

***

## 4. Numbers and Notation

*   [ ] Decimals include a **leading zero**  
    *(0.01, not .01)*
*   [ ] Scientific notation uses **×10ⁿ format**  
    *(e.g.,* *3.7 × 10⁵*)\*
*   [ ] `e` or `E` notation is **not used** for powers of ten

***

## 5. Units and Measurements

*   [ ] **Space between number and unit**  
    *(5 m, 10 s)*
*   [ ] **No space** before `%` or angle degrees  
    *(5%, 90°)*
*   [ ] **SI units used** where possible
*   [ ] Compound units use a **middle dot**  
    *(m·s⁻¹)*
*   [ ] Unit formatting is **consistent throughout**

***

## 6. Equations (General)

*   [ ] Equations appear **inline or displayed**, as appropriate
*   [ ] Display equations are **numbered sequentially**  
    *(Equation (1), (2), … — no section numbers)*
*   [ ] Appendix equations use **A‑prefix** *(Equation (A1))*
*   [ ] Supplementary equations use **S‑prefix** *(Equation (S1))*
*   [ ] **Subequations avoided** where possible; if used, labeled *(1a), (1b)*

***

## 7. Equation Integration and Punctuation

*   [ ] Equations are treated as **part of sentences**
*   [ ] Correct punctuation follows equations (period if sentence ends)
*   [ ] Colon before equations is **optional**, not mandatory
*   [ ] Symbol definitions follow equations using **“where” (lowercase)**  
    – flush left  
    – not a new paragraph

***

## 8. Symbol Definitions and Consistency

*   [ ] **All symbols are defined** in the text
*   [ ] No symbol is used with **multiple meanings**
*   [ ] **Standard notation** is used where it exists in the field

***

## 9. Equation Editing and Submission

### Microsoft Word

*   [ ] Equations created with **Word Equation Editor**
*   [ ] **No equations as images**
*   [ ] All equations are **fully editable**

### LaTeX

*   [ ] Uses MDPI class file
*   [ ] Common packages used (`amsmath`, `amsthm`)
*   [ ] Symbols rely on standard LaTeX packages

***

## 10. Mathematical Environments (If Applicable)

*   [ ] Theorem‑style environments used **only when appropriate**
*   [ ] Supported environments are correctly labeled and numbered:
    *   Theorem, Lemma, Corollary, Proposition
    *   Definition, Remark, Example, Hypothesis
*   [ ] Proofs use a **Proof** environment
*   [ ] Proof references are explicit if not immediately following result

***

## ✅ Final Pre‑Submission Check

*   [ ] Mathematical notation is **clear, consistent, and unambiguous**
*   [ ] All equations are **readable without referring to figures**
*   [ ] Formatting matches MDPI conventions and templates
*   [ ] No mathematical symbols are embedded as images
